About TQM

A trading partner that understands paper making

TQM specialises in trading paper, tissue and waste paper of all kinds. We act as the trading arm of Quality Masters Group, so our customers benefit from the group's manufacturing knowledge, not just a price list.

The quality of our service rests on three things:

Technical know-how

The parent group's experience in paper manufacturing, and full knowledge of the quality standards in paper production.

Shipping & customs

Our team brings hands-on experience in international shipping and customs clearance around the world.

International network

A network of trade relationships across Asia, Africa and Europe that lets us meet customer needs quickly and well.
